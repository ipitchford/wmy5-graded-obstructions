# Sources and dependency access

The manuscript bibliography and [sources.json](sources.json) give the source
record. This is a targeted dependency map, not exhaustive priority certification.

- liqsweep / blueberryvertigo, *An integer polynomial Hurwitz counterexample in
  dimension six*: https://github.com/blueberryvertigo/ArXiv-tryouts . Baseline
  formulas; the release independently expands the characteristic identity and
  checks the three listed zeros.
- Castañeda, Honorato and Valenzuela-Henríquez, *The weak Markus--Yamabe
  conjecture fails in dimension 14*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05392 . Chain
  construction and prior shortening context.
- Meng and Yang, *A five-variable counterexample to the Hessian conjecture* v2:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22198v2 . Audited Hessian construction. The theorem
  here is stated independently of the numerical coefficient of its linear term.
- Korea Superintelligence Labs, *The degree filtration of the Alpoge
  subalgebra*: https://ideosphere.ai/papers/markus-yamabe-dim-v1.pdf . A distinct
  degree-bounded retargeting obstruction.
- Liu, *On the Hessian conjecture in Lorentzian signature: Constant pivots and
  Hesse systems*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19112 . Hesse-system terminology and
  coefficient-span context.

Referenced works are linked rather than bundled or relicensed.
