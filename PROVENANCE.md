# Provenance

The starting research package is the 14 October 2026 WMY5 obstruction bundle,
supplied with an 18 October 2026 AI-assisted review and evidence package.
Revision on 19 October 2026 retained Anonymous as scholarly creator and kept
private editorial material outside the public research payload.

Substantive revisions made the two bigraded architecture inventories explicit,
replaced the final chain certificate-only presentation with a short exact linear
contradiction, strengthened the fixed-potential Hessian calculation to a uniform
affine-in-auxiliary family, and sharpened prior-work and open-boundary language.
The supplied formulas, exact checker and certificates were preserved and rerun.

AI-assisted mathematical revision, checking and editorial review do not
constitute external human peer review. Account ownership and publication
operation are not scholarly authorship. Private rating language is not part of
this export.

The version-of-record package is identified by DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.23477226`; its public source repository is
<https://github.com/ipitchford/wmy5-graded-obstructions>.
